 General information 
Blaze a trail and heat up the milky way with all new P.E.T. equipment that gets stronger at higher HEAT levels. Also, introducing Overheat level to selected existing gears!
 - With this update, we’re presenting an improvement of the P.E.T. enemy locator – this is a wish from our community for a better usability. The enemy locator will now sort all enemies alphabetically.
- We are also introducing new P.E.T. equipment such as a new P.E.T. weapon, a new P.E.T. generator and a new P.E.T. gear.
- On top, we also introduce a new „Overheat“ level – the Level X that you can get for some P.E.T. gear. If the P.E.T. is at a higher HEAT Level, the gear with Level X will perform better.
​
This P.E.T. expansion improves the existing P.E.T. feature and provides even more strategic gameplay with the help of your P.E.T.
 Improved P.E.T. enemy locator 
We got the feedback from our community that it’s very hard to find a desired enemy in the P.E.T. enemy locator. Therefore, we did some changes in the background for the enemy list so that now the enemies are sorted in alphabetical order.
 New P.E.T. Items 
The new P.E.T. items can only be equipped on your P.E.T. and will only be shown in the P.E.T. tab of your hangar.
 P.E.T. Gear G-HR1 
Activating this P.E.T. gear consumes P.E.T. HP to reduce HEAT and damage surrounding enemies. Will bypass cooldown delay when reducing HEAT to lower HEAT levels.
This gear offers you a way to play at high Heat levels, yet at the same time reduce the amount of time spent on high HEAT Levels to save costs.
Different effects:
 1. This gear deals damage to enemies within a certain range
2. This gear releases HEAT and can bypass the cooldown delay to the lower level
3. It inflicts self damage on the P.E.T.​
After the effect is over, the P.E.T. reverts its behaviour to „Guard“ mode.
You get the first 3 levels of this gear in the shop for Uridium, the Level 3X is available via special sales.
Level 3X is an Overheat level – the higher the HEAT level of the P.E.T., the stronger its effect.
P.E.T. Laser LF-PX01 
Small laser designed especially for P.E.T.s: Causes up to 280 damage points per round. HEAT levels will improve the damage output.
 - The base stats are 280 Damage, 600 units Range and 80 Hit chance.
- With each upgrade level, there’s a bonus of 0.5%.
- Each HEAT Level gives a Damage bonus:
 o Level 0: base stats
o Level 1: 0.5%
o Level 2: 1.0%
o Level 3: 2.0%
o Level 4: 4.0%
o Level 5: 8.0%
​
This laser can only be equipped on P.E.T. and you an get it via special sales or with a chance as a reward during Bonus GG Rewards event. You can sell it for 25,000 Credits.
 P.E.T. Shield SG3N-PX01 
A compact shield generator designed especially for P.E.T.s: 11,900 shield strength / 80% less damage. HEAT levels will improve the shield strength.
 - The base stats are 11,900 shield strength, and 80 spread.
- With each upgrade level, there’s a bonus of 1.0%.
- Each HEAT Level gives a Shield bonus:
 o Level 0: base stats
o Level 1: 0.5%
o Level 2: 1.0%
o Level 3: 2.0%
o Level 4: 4.0%
o Level 5: 8.0%​
This shield can only be equipped on P.E.T. and you an get it via special sales or with a chance as a reward during Bonus GG Rewards event.
 Hotkey Toolbar 
The new P.E.T gear has been added to the P.E.T. section in the hotkey toolbar. There’s an arrow to scroll to the right. The hotkey toolbar can only be seen if you have a P.E.T. and the new gear can only be seen if your P.E.T. has them equipped.
 ​
When clicking on the P.E.T. symbol, the gear appears in the hotkey toolbar.
 ​
The following P.E.T. gear will be shown in the hotkey toolbar:
 - G-FS1
- G-HPL1
- G-KK1
- G-MM1
- G-RT1
- G-HR1 (new item)​
 Please note: All pet gears will share a global cooldown timer, and active gear cannot be switched out while on cooldown. 
New P.E.T. Overheat Levels – Level X 
Some of the existing P.E.T. gears will be updated to have a new level – Level X also known as the Overheat Level. These gears will have the same base values as the associated gear Level. For example, the base stats for a Level 3X G-KK are the same as the base stats of a Level 3 G-KK.
The advantage of the Overheat-capable gear in comparison to regular gears is that its effect will be stronger when the P.E.T. is operating at higher HEAT levels.
 P.E.T. Gear G-EL3X (Level 3X) 
 Enemy Locator 
Effect: Gives you the ability to locate a single alien within a certain range. It can be upgraded with protocols. With each level (1 – 3) the range increases.
 Enemy Locator Level 3X 
Effect: Like the regular Enemy locator but with 3X level, the reach in each HEAT Level gets even bigger than before with normal HEAT. With higher HEAT levels, the range is increased further.
Stats:
 - At HEAT Level 0 (the default level), the Enemy Locator Level 3X can detect enemies as far as 5,000 unit radius away from the P.E.T.
- At HEAT Level 5 (which is the highest), the Enemy Locator Level 3X can detect enemies as far as 5,900 unit radius away from the P.E.T.
​
You get it in the shop for 50,000 Uridium and you can sell it for 125,000 Credits.
 P.E.T. Gear G-AL3X (Level 3X) 
 Auto-Loot equipment 
Effect: Once you activate this P.E.T. gear, your P.E.T. will automatically collect bonus boxes and cargo boxes within a close range. With each level (1 – 3) the range increases.
 Auto-Loot equipment 3X 
Effect: Like the regular Auto-Loot gear but with each HEAT Level, the range increases.
Stats:
 - At HEAT Level 0 (the default level), the Auto-Loot equipment 3X collect bonus boxes and cargo boxes within a range of 3,000 units.
- At HEAT Level 5 (which is the highest), the Auto-Loot equipment Level 3X collect bonus boxes and cargo boxes within a range of 3,540 units.
​
You can get it via special sales and/or events. You can sell it for 125,000 Credits.
 P.E.T. Gear G-AR3X (Level 3X) 
 Auto-Resource Collector 
Once you activate this P.E.T. gear, your P.E.T. will automatically collect resources within a close range. With each level (1 – 3) the range increases.
 Auto-Resource Collector 3X 
Effect: Like the regular Auto-Resource Collector gear but with each HEAT Level, the range increases.
Stats:
 - At HEAT Level 0 (the default level), the Auto-Resource Collector 3X collect resources within a range of 3,000 units.
- At HEAT Level 5 (which is the highest), the Auto-Resource Collector Level 3X collect resources within a range of 3,540 units.
​
You can get it via special sales and/or events. You can sell it for 62,500 Credits.
 P.E.T. Gear G-RL3X (Level 3X) 
 Resource Locator 
With this gear on board, your P.E.T. will be able to track down nearby resources in the current star system.
 Resource Locator 3X 
Effect: Like the regular Resource Locator gear but with each HEAT Level, the range increases.
Stats:
 - At HEAT Level 0 (the default level), the Resource Locator 3X tracks down resources within a range of 5,000 units.
- At HEAT Level 5 (which is the highest), the Resource Locator Level 3X tracks down resources within a range of 5,900 units.
​
You can get it in the shop for 25,000 Uridium. You can sell it for 62,500 Credits.
